What we collect and why

Mailing list

When you leave your email address in the form on our website, we store it so that we can send you a message when LINKas launches. We don’t use your address for anything else or pass it on. We process it on the basis of your consent.

Along with your address, we store the language of the page you signed up on (Finnish or English), so that the launch message reaches you in the right language. The addresses are stored in Google Forms. We delete them once the launch message has been sent.

Visitor statistics (Google Analytics)

If you accept cookies, we use Google Analytics to see how many people visit the site and how it is used, so that we can improve it. Google Analytics collects information such as the pages viewed, where visitors came from, the type of device and browser, and approximate location at country or city level. Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses. We do not link this information to you as a person, and we do not use it for advertising.

We process this on the basis of your consent. If you don’t accept cookies, Google Analytics does not start at all. Google Analytics keeps individual event data for 2 months.

The service is provided by Google Ireland Limited. Data may be transferred to the United States; Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Cookies

  • _ga and _ga_44M3K88PF2 (Google Analytics): visitor statistics. Only set if you accept cookies. Valid for up to 2 years.
  • Your cookie choice is saved in your browser’s own storage so that the notice doesn’t appear on every visit. This information never leaves your browser.

You can change your choice at any time.

Other services

The website runs on Cloudflare, which processes the technical information needed to deliver the page, such as your IP address. The site’s fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser’s IP address is passed to Google so that the fonts can load.

Your rights

You have the right of access to the information we hold about you, the right to have it corrected or deleted, and the right to withdraw your consent. Contact us at hello@linkas.app. If you think your information is being processed unlawfully, you can lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman in Finland (tietosuoja.fi).
