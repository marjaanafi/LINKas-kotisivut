How it works

Nobody has to remember it all on their own.

LINKas is a shared calendar app for families. It brings your kids’ activities, the school timetable, chores, shopping lists and school bus times together in one place. There are two views: you see the whole family’s plans, tasks and lists, and your child sees their own plans and jobs, laid out simply enough for a primary school child to check alone. After a while, you’ll hardly ever hear “Is there training tonight?” at home.

1

Connect the calendars once, and they stay up to date on their own

Connect your kids’ club calendars and bring in their timetable from Wilma, the school app many Finnish schools use. Both stay up to date on their own: when the coach moves a training session, it moves in LINKas too. You can add everything else however suits you best: type it, take a photo of a message, or just say it out loud.

2

Hand out the week’s chores in seconds

Say it all in one go:

“Ella vie pullot kauppaan torstaina ja Otto tyhjentää tiskikoneen lauantaina.”Ella returns the bottles to the shop on Thursday, and Otto empties the dishwasher on Saturday.

LINKas turns it into tasks for the right children on the right days. One recording can hand out several tasks to several people in the family.

3

Everyone in the family sees their own plans and jobs

Your child opens their own view and sees today’s plans, their own tasks and which bus will get them there in time. You see the whole family. The reminder about homework disappears as soon as your child ticks it off.

4

Put extra jobs on offer, and your child picks their own

You might offer raking the leaves for €5, or washing the windows for an hour of screen time. Your child chooses what they want to do, and you sign the job off once it’s done. LINKas keeps a running total for the week, and you hand over the money yourself.

What’s inside

A family calendar, and everything else your family needs to remember.

Calendar

A family calendar with your kids’ activities and everyone’s plans in one view. Club calendars update automatically: any calendar with an iCal link works.

School timetable

Your child’s timetable from Wilma, straight into the app and kept up to date. Or take a screenshot of it and LINKas reads it.

Tasks

A family to-do list where you can type a task, snap a photo of it or say it out loud. One recording can hand out several tasks to several people.

Reminders

A reminder stays on screen until your child ticks the task off.

Buses

Set your home stop and the school stop, and LINKas shows the bus times for the school run: which bus to take and when to leave. Works anywhere in Finland.

Lists

Shopping list, to-do list, favourite recipes or any other shared list. Share it with the whole family or just the adults. It updates for everyone straight away.

Recipes

Take a photo or screenshot of a recipe, and LINKas saves it to your lists. If the picture shows where the recipe came from, LINKas keeps a note of the source.

Extras

Put extra jobs on offer, and your child picks the ones they’d like to do. The reward can be money or screen time. You can even sign off a job while you’re at work, once your child has added a photo.

Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the most common questions.

What is LINKas?

LINKas is a shared family calendar app. It brings together your kids’ activities, the school timetable, chores, school bus times and shared lists, such as shopping and to-do lists. Children and parents have their own views, and children can earn money or screen time by doing extras.

Is LINKas available in English?

No, the app is in Finnish. Most of what you see day to day is names, times and places, but we know that isn’t the same as having it in English.

When does LINKas launch?

In late 2026, on the App Store and Google Play. Leave your email and we’ll send you one message on launch day.

How much does LINKas cost?

The first month is free. After that it’s €4.99 a month for the whole family. The price includes VAT and doesn’t depend on how many people are in your family.

Does LINKas work with my kids’ club calendars?

Yes. Any club or activity calendar that gives you an iCal link updates in LINKas automatically.

Does LINKas work with Wilma?

Yes. Wilma is the school app many Finnish schools use. Bring your child’s timetable in from Wilma and it stays up to date on its own, or take a screenshot of it and LINKas reads it.

Does my child need their own phone?

No. Your child’s view works on their own phone or on a shared family tablet.

Can my child earn money or screen time?

Yes, with extras. You put extra jobs on offer, like raking the leaves for €5 or washing the windows for an hour of screen time. Your child picks the ones they want, marks them as done and can add a photo. Once you approve the job, the reward is added to your child’s total for the week.

Do bus suggestions work everywhere in Finland?

Yes, anywhere in Finland.

What if my child lives in two homes?

Your child has one view that shows their plans in both homes. Each home uses LINKas with its own subscription.
